Who We Are

Bringing Fabric to Life.

Welcome to PRINT INDIA, where textile artistry and tradition have been our guiding principles for over three decades. With 30 years of experience, we have built our expertise around vibrant colours, intricate designs and quality printing.

Our journey began with a passion for quality and a commitment to preserving the rich heritage of Indian textile printing. Over the years, this passion has grown into a trusted name for creative and specialised printing services.

Founded by Salam Bhai, PRINT INDIA continues to carry forward a legacy built on craftsmanship, dedication and attention to detail.
